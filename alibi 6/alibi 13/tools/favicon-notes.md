# Yellow smiley favicon

The source smiley was edited with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized for the site's favicon assets. The yellow face retains the charcoal, hand-scribbled outline and has a transparent exterior. The tab uses `public/favicon-yellow.png` at 64 × 64; the previous WebP and SVG paths also contain the updated smiley for compatibility.

Actual transparent alpha outside the circular face. No white or black rectangular background. No text, shadow, border beyond the existing circle, extra decorations, or additional faces.
